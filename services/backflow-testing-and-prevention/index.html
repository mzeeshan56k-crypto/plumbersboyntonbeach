Who Needs Annual Backflow Testing in Palm Beach County

If any of these apply to your property, an annual test is almost certainly required.

How it works

What a Backflow Preventer Does and Why the Test Is Required

The rule exists because water does not always flow the direction you assume it does.

The failure it prevents

When a water main breaks or a hydrant is opened for firefighting, pressure in the street can drop below pressure inside your property. Without a working preventer, water sitting in an irrigation line, a boiler or a chemical injector can be drawn backward into the public drinking supply. Fertilizer, pool chemicals and stagnant irrigation water are the usual contaminants.

Device types

A double check valve assembly suits lower-hazard connections. A reduced pressure zone assembly, or RPZ, includes a relief valve that dumps water if both checks fail and is required on higher-hazard connections. Pressure vacuum breakers are common on residential irrigation.

What the test measures

Using calibrated gauges, we verify that each check valve holds against reverse flow and that the relief valve opens at the correct differential pressure. Marginal readings fail, because a device that barely holds today will not hold next season.

Why devices fail here

Sandy soil and heavy irrigation push grit into check seats. Constant sun degrades rubber faster than in cooler climates. Salt air corrodes exposed brass on coastal properties. A device that passed comfortably two years ago can fail on relief pressure now.

Before you call

What to Have Ready for a Backflow Test

The test itself takes under half an hour. Having these to hand keeps it that way.

  • Find your testing notice; it carries the device and account numbers.
  • Locate the assembly, usually near the meter or at the irrigation point of connection.
  • Clear vegetation and mulch away so the test cocks are reachable.
  • Let anyone on site know the water will be off for a few minutes.

One thing not to do: Do not attempt to adjust or rebuild an assembly yourself. A device that has been tampered with fails its certification and has to be replaced.

Urgency

When Is Backflow an Urgent Problem?

Annual testing is scheduled work. It becomes urgent if:

  • The assembly is discharging water continuously from the relief port.
  • You have been notified that your water service will be shut off for non-compliance.
  • Irrigation or pool water may have been drawn back into the household supply.
  • The device has been struck, frozen or visibly damaged.

If any of those describe what you are seeing, call (833) 567-2788 now rather than booking online. Emergency calls are moved to the front of the queue.

FAQs

Backflow Testing FAQs

How often is testing required?

Annually for most assemblies in Boynton Beach and Palm Beach County. Some commercial and fire systems are tested more often.

How long does the test take?

About 20 to 30 minutes per device. Water to the irrigation system is off briefly during the test.

What happens if my device fails?

We quote a rebuild on the spot. Most failures are worn rubber parts and are fixed the same visit, then retested.

Do you file the paperwork?

Yes. The report goes to your water provider and a copy is emailed to you for your records.

Can I skip the test if my irrigation is off?

No. The requirement follows the connection, not usage. An unused assembly still needs certification.

What happens if I miss the annual backflow test?

The water provider issues a notice, and continued non-compliance can lead to the irrigation connection or the water service being shut off. The test is short and inexpensive compared with that, so the sensible move is to book it when the notice arrives rather than when the deadline passes.
