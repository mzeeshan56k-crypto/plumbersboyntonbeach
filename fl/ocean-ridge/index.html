Ocean Ridge is a small town of mostly single-family homes between the ocean and the Intracoastal, many built decades ago and renovated several times since. Knowing what is behind the walls before opening them matters here.

Island conditions

Salt, Humidity and the Water Table

Three forces work against plumbing on the island. Salt air corrodes anything exposed. Constant humidity keeps utility spaces damp, which rusts water heater bases and supports. And the water table sits close to the surface, so anything buried is effectively in wet soil year round.

That last point matters most for sewer laterals and irrigation lines. Buried metal degrades fast, and a leak in saturated sand shows no surface sign at all. We pressure test rather than guess.

Inside the house, the practical advice is the same as everywhere on the coast: solid brass fixtures, stainless hardware, a drain pan under every water heater, and an annual look at anything exposed to outside air.

Renovated homes

Mixed Pipe in Renovated Beach Houses

Most Ocean Ridge homes have been renovated at least once, sometimes three or four times. Each renovation added a new section of pipe in whatever material was standard at the time, so it is common to find galvanized, copper, CPVC and PEX all in the same house.

Mixed systems create their own problems, particularly where dissimilar metals meet without a dielectric union and corrode at the joint. Before quoting any significant work we map what is actually installed, so the repair addresses the real system rather than an assumed one.

Seasonal homes

Protecting an Ocean Ridge Home While You Are Away

Many homes here sit empty for part of the year, and an unattended leak is the single most expensive thing that can happen to them.

Shut the water off at the main. A failed supply line in an empty house can run for weeks. Closing one valve removes that risk entirely, and it costs nothing.

Consider an automatic shutoff valve. Leak detection valves monitor flow and close the water on their own when they see a pattern that looks like a leak. For a seasonal property, this is the highest-value plumbing upgrade available.

Water heater on vacation mode. Turn it down or off rather than heating an empty house for months, which also slows scale buildup inside the tank.

Before you leave and when you return. Have exposed fittings, the heater pan, angle stops and irrigation checked once a year, ideally ahead of hurricane season, and flush the system when you come back.

Emergency Plumber

When a supply line lets go or sewage comes up through a floor drain, the first job is stopping the water and the second is telling you what the repair costs. Our emergency plumbers in Boynton Beach walk you through the shutoff on the phone, arrive with a stocked truck, and hand you a written flat price before anything is opened up.

Drain Cleaning

Slow showers, gurgling sinks and a main line that backs up every few months are rarely one hair clog. They are grease, scale and old cast iron that has narrowed over decades. We clear the blockage with cabling or hydro jetting, then camera the pipe so you can see whether it was a clog or a damaged line causing it.

Water Heater Repair & Installation

No hot water, rusty hot water or a puddle under the tank each point somewhere different. We test elements, thermostats, gas valves and the anode rod before quoting, because a failed part costs a fraction of a new heater. When the tank really is finished, we replace 40 to 80 gallon units with the permit and haul-away included.

Tankless Water Heater Installation

A tankless water heater ends the back-to-back shower problem and frees up the closet or garage floor. Getting twenty years out of one depends on demand sizing, gas or electrical capacity and descaling access, not the brand on the box. We size the unit to your real fixture load and build in isolation valves for annual flushing.

Leak Detection and Repair

A water bill that doubled with nothing visible means water is escaping somewhere you cannot see. Acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and pressure isolation let us pinpoint hidden leaks in walls, slabs, irrigation and pool lines, usually within inches. Demolition is the last resort here, never the opening move.

Slab Leak Repair

Warm patches on a tile floor, running water with every tap closed and hairline cracks across a room all point under the concrete. Boynton Beach homes sit on slabs with copper poured into them, so this is one of our most common calls. We locate the leak precisely, then price spot repair, overhead reroute and repipe side by side.

FAQs

Ocean Ridge Plumbing Questions, Answered

Do you serve the barrier island?

Yes, all of Ocean Ridge from the ocean side to the Intracoastal, with the same-day scheduling we run on the mainland.

Why do my pipes corrode faster here?

Salt air, high humidity and a shallow water table. Buried and exposed metal both degrade faster on the island than inland.

Can you find a leak without digging up my yard?

Usually. Acoustic and pressure testing pinpoint most leaks, so any excavation is small and targeted.

Do you handle whole-house repipes on the island?

Yes, in PEX or copper, permitted and inspected, typically over two to four days.
