Two building eras

East Boca Cast Iron, West Boca Systems

East Boca Raton, including the neighborhoods around Old Floresta, Boca Villas and the Golden Triangle, is largely mid-century. Those homes have cast iron drains that are now sixty years old, and the failure pattern is predictable: the bottom channel of the pipe rusts away and the line starts holding water.

The result is repeat main line backups that no amount of cabling fixes for long. We camera the lateral first, show you the footage, and then price lining or replacement against another year of clearings.

West Boca is a different world. Homes in communities like Boca Falls, Boca Isles and Loggers Run are 1990s and 2000s builds with CPVC or PEX. Those calls are mostly water heaters, pressure regulators, filtration systems and irrigation backflow testing rather than failing pipe.

High-end fixtures

Luxury Fixtures Need the Right Plumber

Boca Raton homes carry a lot of imported and specialty plumbing: thermostatic shower systems with multiple body sprays, freestanding tub fillers, steam showers, wine room chillers and outdoor kitchens. These need parts and knowledge that a general handyman rarely has on the truck.

We identify the valve body and source the correct cartridge rather than replacing an entire system because a five dollar seal failed. Where a fixture is discontinued, we say so early and price the realistic options instead of chasing parts for weeks.

Good to know

Boca Raton Plumbing: Utilities, Housing and Local Requirements

Boca Raton covers city addresses and a large unincorporated western area, and the details differ between them.

City utilities and west Boca service

Boca Raton Utility Services covers city addresses, while much of west Boca is served by Palm Beach County Water Utilities. That distinction determines where permits are filed and where backflow reports are submitted. It also affects reclaimed water availability, since parts of the city irrigate with reclaimed supply under strict cross-connection rules that require annual certified testing.

East and west building stock

East Boca neighborhoods such as Old Floresta, Boca Villas and the Golden Triangle are mid-century, with sixty-year-old cast iron laterals and slab-routed copper. West Boca communities including Boca Falls, Boca Isles and Loggers Run are 1990s and 2000s builds on CPVC or PEX. The eastern homes call us about pipe failure and sewer lines; the western ones about water heaters, pressure regulators and filtration.

Gated communities and HOA access

A large share of Boca Raton properties sit inside gated or country club communities with their own access procedures, approved vendor requirements and work-hour restrictions. We handle gate authorization, provide licensing and insurance documentation to management in advance, and schedule around community rules so a booked appointment is not turned away at the gate.

What Should Boca Raton Homeowners Check Themselves?

Nothing here needs a trade license, and each item is a failure we are regularly called to too late in Boca.

  • Check the irrigation backflow device for your annual testing notice.
  • Walk the floor for warm patches, particularly in homes built before 1990.
  • Look under sinks for corrosion on the angle stops and supply lines.
  • Read the meter with everything off if the bill has moved without explanation.
  • In a condo, find out where your unit's shutoff is and whether you can reach it.

What Plumbing Work Do We Do in Boca Raton?

Every service below is available at Boca Raton addresses, on the same schedule and the same flat-rate pricing we use in Boynton Beach.

Emergency Plumber

When a supply line lets go or sewage backs up through a floor drain, the first job is stopping the water and the second is telling you what the repair costs. We walk you through the shutoff on the phone, arrive with a stocked truck, and hand you a written flat price before anything is opened up. The 1970s and 1980s homes in Boca Raton Square and Boca Del Mar carry most of the aging slab copper, while 33428 and 33498 are newer and fail differently.

Drain Cleaning

A drain that clears and re-clogs within weeks is telling you the pipe itself is the problem. We cable or hydro jet the line to get flow back, then run a camera so you can see whether you are dealing with a clog, a belly or a break. West Boca's newer homes in 33428 and 33498 fail at fittings and manifolds rather than in the pipe itself.

Water Heater Repair & Installation

No hot water, rusty hot water or a puddle under the tank each point somewhere different. We test elements, thermostats, gas valves and the anode rod before quoting, because a failed part costs a fraction of a new heater. When the tank really is finished, we replace 40 to 80 gallon units with the permit and haul-away included. Condo installs along A1A come with building rules on shutoffs and access, and we clear those before the truck arrives.

Tankless Water Heater Installation

Going tankless works when the gas line, venting and electrical capacity are right for the unit. We measure your actual fixture demand first, then install with isolation valves so annual descaling is a short service call rather than a teardown. Larger Boca homes often run two heaters or a recirculation loop, and we balance the system rather than oversize it.

Leak Detection and Repair

A water bill that doubled with nothing visible means water is escaping somewhere you cannot see. Acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and pressure isolation let us pinpoint hidden leaks in walls, slabs, irrigation and pool lines, usually within inches. Demolition is the last resort here, never the opening move. Central Boca homes from the 1970s are the ones where slab copper is now reaching the end of its life.

Slab Leak Repair

A slab leak shows up as a warm spot underfoot, a meter that turns with everything off, or a crack that keeps widening. We pinpoint it without opening the floor first, then quote the three honest options: spot repair, overhead reroute, or repipe. Condo buildings along the coast run shared stacks, so one unit's blockage is often a building issue.

Sewer Line Repair and Replacement

Repeat main line backups, sewage smell in the yard and a soggy strip of lawn mean the lateral itself has failed. We run a recorded camera inspection the full length of the pipe, locate every defect and its depth, then quote spot repair, trenchless lining or full replacement based on what the footage actually shows. West Boca's newer construction fails at manifolds and fittings rather than along the pipe.

Pipe Repair and Installation

We repair single failures and repipe whole houses in copper, CPVC or PEX. Runs go through the attic and walls with the smallest openings that will do the job, and every line is pressure tested and inspected before the drywall is closed back up. Boca's larger lots mean longer irrigation and pool runs, which widens where a hidden leak can hide.

Toilet Repair and Installation

Most toilet complaints are a flapper, a fill valve or a wax ring, and all three are same-visit fixes. The ones worth taking seriously are water at the base and a bowl that rocks, because both mean the flange seal is leaking into your subfloor. We repair, reseal or install new comfort-height units. Rental turnovers near Florida Atlantic University and Mizner Park need fast, clean fixture swaps scheduled around building access.

Faucet & Fixture Installation

We set faucets, shower valves, tub spouts and bidet seats, and we replace the angle stops and supply lines while we are in there. High-end and imported trim is common here, so we source the correct parts instead of substituting.

Kitchen and Bathroom Plumbing

Remodel plumbing runs in two stages: rough-in while the walls are open, then trim once the tile and cabinets are set. Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation

Humming means jammed, silence means power, and a drip from the bottom seam means the motor seal is gone. Imported and high-end trim is common in Boca, so we source correct parts rather than substituting.

New gas runs for ranges, dryers, pool heaters, generators and outdoor kitchens are sized to the appliance load, pressure tested and permitted. Backflow Testing and Prevention

Water Filtration Installation

Sump Pump Installation and Repair

Do you serve west Boca Raton and unincorporated areas?
