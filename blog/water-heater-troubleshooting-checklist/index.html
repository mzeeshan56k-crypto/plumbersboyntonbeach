No Hot Water? A Boynton Beach Homeowner Checklist Before You Call

Six things to check before calling a plumber about a water heater, and the three symptoms that mean stop and call now. If the checklist does not solve it, water heater repair covers what happens next, and tankless installation is worth reading if the tank is finished. Both are available across our service areas.

Published July 21, 2026 · Plumbers Boynton Beach

No hot water is a bad start to a morning, and it is not always a plumber problem. Work through this checklist first. It takes ten minutes and it sometimes saves the cost of a call.

Stop and call immediately if you see any of these

Three symptoms mean stop troubleshooting and pick up the phone.

Water pooling under the tank. If it is coming from the tank body rather than a fitting, the tank has failed and it will not be repaired. Shut off the water supply to the heater and the power or gas.

A gas smell. Leave the house, do not touch switches, and call the gas utility from outside.

The relief valve discharging steadily. That valve exists to prevent a tank from becoming dangerous. If it is dumping water, something is wrong with pressure or temperature. Do not cap it or plug it.

Electric heaters: six things to check

1. The breaker

Check the panel. A water heater breaker can trip without looking obviously off. Flip it fully off, then back on. If it trips again, stop. A repeatedly tripping breaker means a shorted element or wiring fault.

2. The reset button

Behind the upper access panel there is a red high-limit reset button. Press it. If it clicks and hot water returns, note the date. If it trips again within weeks, a thermostat or element is failing.

3. The thermostat setting

Someone may have turned it down, or a cleaning service may have bumped it. The standard setting is 120 degrees. Higher than 125 is a scald risk, especially with children in the house.

4. Recent heavy use

Three showers, a dishwasher and a load of laundry in ninety minutes will empty any tank. If hot water returns after an hour of no use, the heater is working and the tank is simply undersized for your peak demand.

5. Sediment buildup

Rumbling or popping while heating means calcium has settled on the tank floor. On an electric heater it buries the lower element, so you get a fraction of the normal hot water. A flush often restores real capacity.

6. Age

Find the serial number on the data plate. Most manufacturers encode the year in the first four characters. Past ten years in Palm Beach County water, repair stops being the better spend.

Gas heaters: what is different

Start with the pilot or ignition. On older units, check whether the pilot light is out and relight it following the label instructions. If it will not stay lit, the thermocouple is usually the reason and it is an inexpensive part.

On newer sealed-combustion units there is no standing pilot. Look for an error code on the control module and check that the gas valve at the appliance is open. Also make sure the venting is clear and nothing is stacked against the air intake.

Yellow or orange flames rather than blue mean incomplete combustion. That is worth a service call promptly.

Why water heaters fail early here

Palm Beach County water is hard, commonly 8 to 12 grains per gallon. Every gallon leaves a little calcium behind, and it accumulates on the bottom of the tank.

On a gas heater, that sediment layer insulates the burner from the water, so the steel overheats and eventually cracks. On an electric heater, it buries the lower element until it burns out. Either way, a tank rated for twelve years commonly dies here in eight.

Two habits change that outcome. Flush the tank annually, and replace the anode rod every three to five years. The anode is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the tank does not. Once it is consumed, the tank starts corroding instead. Replacing a rod is far cheaper than replacing a heater.

Garage installations and Florida code

Many Boynton Beach homes put the water heater in an attached garage. Florida code has specific requirements there, including elevation or an approved sealed unit for gas models, and a drain pan piped to the exterior.

We see plenty of older installations missing the pan entirely. That is the difference between a failed tank being an inconvenience and a failed tank being a flooded garage. If yours has no pan, add one at the next service.

Repair or replace

Under eight years old with a sound tank and a failed component, repair is the right call. An element, thermostat, thermocouple or gas control valve all cost a fraction of a new unit.

Past twelve years, or with any sign of tank corrosion, replacement is the better buy. Between eight and twelve, it depends on the failed part and the condition of the tank. A good plumber will price both and let you decide with real numbers.

Before you call

Have three pieces of information ready: the age from the data plate, whether it is gas or electric, and the tank capacity in gallons. That is enough for an accurate price range on the phone, and it makes the visit faster.
