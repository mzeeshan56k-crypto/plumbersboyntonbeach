Why Your Water Bill Suddenly Doubled

Most of the time it is one of five things, and two of them are cheap.

A high water bill in Boynton Beach almost always has a physical cause. Water use does not jump on its own, so if nothing changed in the household, something is leaking. The job is working out which of a short list of causes applies to you.

Start with the cheap ones. A running toilet can waste thousands of gallons a month and costs very little to repair. Only once those are ruled out is it worth bringing in locating equipment.

The usual causes, cheapest first

Where the water is going

  • Running toilet. How to check: Food coloring in the tank, look for color in the bowl Typical fix: Flapper or fill valve
  • Irrigation leak or stuck valve. How to check: Isolate irrigation and repeat the meter test Typical fix: Valve or line repair
  • Dripping fixture or failed stop. How to check: Look under sinks for damp or corrosion Typical fix: Washer, cartridge or angle stop
  • Slab leak. How to check: Warm floor patch, sound of running water Typical fix: Locate then spot repair or reroute
  • Pool autofill. How to check: Isolate it and watch the meter Typical fix: Float valve

Run the meter test before anything else. It takes two hours and tells you whether you are chasing a leak at all.

The toilet is the first suspect

A flapper that does not seat lets water pass continuously and silently. Put a few drops of food coloring in the tank, wait twenty minutes and look in the bowl. Color in the bowl means the flapper is leaking.

This is a same visit repair and the cheapest item on this page. It is also the single most common answer to a bill that climbed without explanation.

When it is not a leak

Occasionally the cause is a meter or billing error, or a seasonal irrigation schedule nobody remembered changing. Compare the reading on your meter with the one on the bill before assuming a fault. If water is visibly escaping, close the main valve first.

If the meter is moving with everything closed, it is a leak. Slab leak signs cover the one that does the most damage if ignored.

Frequently asked questions

How much water can a running toilet waste?

Enough to show clearly on a monthly bill. It runs continuously, so a small flow adds up faster than most people expect.

Could it be the water heater?

Yes, if the relief valve is discharging or the tank is weeping. Look for water around the base and at the discharge pipe.

Does my utility help with leak adjustments?

Many providers offer a one time adjustment once a leak is repaired and documented. Keep the invoice and the written diagnosis.
