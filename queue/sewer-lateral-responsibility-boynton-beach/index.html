Who Owns the Sewer Lateral, You or the City?

In most cases the pipe from your house to the main is yours.

Sewer lateral responsibility in Boynton Beach matters most at the worst possible moment, which is when the line has failed and someone has to pay for it. In Palm Beach County the property owner is generally responsible for the lateral running from the house to the connection at the public main.

The utility owns and maintains the main itself. Exactly where the dividing line sits depends on your municipality, and that is worth establishing before a repair rather than during one.

Where the line usually sits

The lateral starts at your house and runs under the yard to the main, which is typically under the street or an easement. In most arrangements everything up to the connection point is the owner's responsibility.

Some municipalities take responsibility from the property line rather than the main, which changes who pays for the section under the sidewalk and street. Your provider can confirm it.

Why the camera run settles it

A recorded camera inspection gives the distance and depth of every defect. That tells you whether the break sits on your side or past the point where responsibility changes.

It is also what decides the repair itself. What camera footage shows covers how to read it.

What repairs look like on your side

Repair routes for a failed lateral

  • Roots at one joint. Repair: Spot repair or liner Disruption: Small dig or none
  • Single crack or offset. Repair: Spot repair Disruption: One excavation
  • Belly along a run. Repair: Section replacement Disruption: Larger trench
  • Collapse. Repair: Replacement or trenchless lining Disruption: Largest

Roots are the most common cause here. Roots in a sewer line covers what stops them coming back.

Knowing who owns the line matters most when it fails. What to do during a backup and the condition of older cast iron both feed into that.

Frequently asked questions

Does insurance cover a sewer lateral?

Standard policies often exclude it, and some carriers offer a service line endorsement separately. Check before you need it rather than after.

Who do I call first, the city or a plumber?

If waste is backing up into the house, call a plumber. If the problem is clearly in the street, call the utility. A camera run tells you which.

Can I get the lateral inspected before buying a home?

Yes, and on any property built before 1980 it is money well spent. The footage is part of the negotiation.
