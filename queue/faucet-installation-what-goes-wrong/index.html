What Goes Wrong With Faucet Installation

The faucet is rarely the problem. The shutoffs underneath usually are.

Faucet installation in a Boynton Beach home looks simple and usually is. The call-backs we see almost never involve the faucet itself. They involve the parts underneath that were left alone because they were still working on the day.

Angle stops and supply lines are the two that matter. Both are cheap, both are easy to replace while the cabinet is already open, and both are what turn a one hour job into a house wide shutdown when they fail later.

The mistakes that cause call-backs

  • Reusing old angle stops that are seized or weeping.
  • Reusing old braided supply lines rather than fitting new ones.
  • Not checking the hole configuration on the sink before buying.
  • Overtightening plastic nuts on the faucet body.
  • Forgetting the new drain assembly that most faucets expect.

Old shutoffs are the biggest of these. Why angle stops should be replaced covers what they cost you when they are skipped.

What to check before buying

Before you order a faucet

  • Hole count and spacing on the sink deck. Why: A three hole sink will not take a single hole faucet without a plate
  • Whether a drain assembly is included. Why: Many are, some are not, and sizes vary
  • Finish against existing trim. Why: Brushed and polished finishes vary between makers
  • Reach and height against the sink. Why: A tall spout in a shallow sink splashes constantly

In coastal homes, finish durability matters more than it does inland. Salt air pits ordinary trim faster than most people expect.

Doing it properly

Shut off, replace the stops, fit new braided lines, set the faucet, fit the drain, then check for leaks under pressure with the cabinet dry and lined with paper.

If a shutoff will not close, stop. Forcing a seized stop is how a simple job turns into a burst line and a wet kitchen.

The same logic applies to showers. Replacing a shower valve is harder to redo, and hard water is what blocks the aerators afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Should I supply my own faucet?

Either works. If you buy it, check hole spacing and whether the drain assembly is included. If we supply it, the warranty is handled in one place.

How long does a faucet swap take?

Usually under an hour and a half per fixture when the shutoffs cooperate. Seized stops add time.

Why did my new faucet start dripping?

Usually a cartridge seated badly or debris from the line caught in it. Flushing the lines before fitting prevents most of these.
