Water Heater Leaking From the Bottom: What Now?

Where the water comes from decides whether this is a part or a new tank.

A water heater leaking from the bottom in Boynton Beach is one of the few plumbing symptoms that settles the repair or replace question by itself. If the water is coming from the tank body or the bottom seam, the vessel has corroded through and no repair to it is durable.

The good news is that most puddles under a heater are not the tank. Fittings, the relief valve and the drain valve all sit low and all leak in ways that look identical from across a garage floor.

Find the source before you panic

  1. Dry the area completely and lay paper towel around the base.
  2. Check the cold and hot connections at the top for drips running down.
  3. Check the temperature and pressure relief valve and its discharge pipe.
  4. Check the drain valve at the bottom front.
  5. If the paper wets from underneath with all of those dry, it is the tank.

Shut the cold inlet valve on top of the heater if water is flowing rather than seeping. That isolates the appliance without shutting off the whole house.

What each source means

Leak source and outcome

  • Inlet or outlet fitting. Repairable?: Yes Typical fix: Reseal or replace the connection
  • Relief valve. Repairable?: Yes Typical fix: Replace the valve, check pressure
  • Drain valve. Repairable?: Yes Typical fix: Replace the valve
  • Tank body or seam. Repairable?: No Typical fix: Replacement, with permit and haul away

A relief valve that keeps discharging is often a pressure problem rather than a faulty valve, and that points at the house rather than the heater.

If it is the tank

Shut the cold inlet, kill power or gas to the unit, and plan the replacement rather than rushing it. Sizing matters, and so does permitting. Permits for water heater replacement covers what the city expects.

It is also the moment to decide whether the replacement should be a tank at all. Tank compared with tankless walks through the choice.

Frequently asked questions

Can a leaking tank be welded or patched?

No. The steel corrodes from the inside across a wide area, so a patch at one point simply moves the next failure a few inches.

How urgent is it?

If it is seeping, you have time to plan. If it is running, shut the inlet valve and call now. Eighty gallons on a garage floor is a different problem.

Why did it fail with no warning?

There usually was warning. Noise and rusty hot water come first. Water heater noise covers the early signs.
