A Pipe Just Burst: What to Do in the First Five Minutes

Shut the water first. Everything else comes second.

When a pipe bursts in a Boynton Beach home, the amount of damage is decided in the first few minutes. A half inch supply line under pressure moves a lot of water very quickly, and it keeps moving until someone closes a valve.

That is why knowing where your main shutoff is matters more than any other piece of plumbing knowledge. If you are reading this before anything has happened, go and find yours now.

The first five minutes

  1. Close the main shutoff valve. Clockwise to shut.
  2. Open a low tap to drain the pressure left in the lines.
  3. Kill power at the breaker to any circuit near standing water.
  4. Move what you can off the wet floor and lift furniture legs.
  5. Photograph everything before you start cleaning up.
  6. Call a plumber and say a line has burst.

Do not step into standing water that may be in contact with electrics. Shut the breaker first or wait for help to arrive.

If you cannot find the valve

Use the meter valve at the street. Most meter boxes have a valve that can be turned with a meter key or an adjustable wrench, and it shuts the whole property.

Finding and testing your main shutoff walks through the usual locations in a Florida home and why testing it twice a year matters.

After the water is off

Dry the area as quickly as you can. In our humidity, standing water in a wall cavity does not dry on its own, and that is where the secondary damage comes from.

Then work out why it burst. A single failure in newer pipe is bad luck. Repeat failures mean the system is finished, and pinhole leaks in copper covers the usual reason.

Once the water is off and the repair is made, ask why it happened. Repeat failures point at a repipe, and high pressure points at the regulator.

Frequently asked questions

Why do pipes burst in Florida if it never freezes?

Freezing is a northern cause. Here it is corrosion, age, high pressure or a failed fitting. The result is the same and so is the response.

Should I try to patch it myself?

Only as a temporary measure once the water is off. A clamp buys time. It is not a repair.

Will insurance cover the damage?

Sudden accidental discharge is commonly covered while the pipe itself may not be. Photograph everything and call your carrier early.
