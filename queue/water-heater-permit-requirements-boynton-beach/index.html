Do You Need a Permit to Replace a Water Heater?

Yes. And the inspection is what protects you if it goes wrong later.

A water heater permit in Boynton Beach is required for replacement, not just for new installation. The permit covers the connections, the venting on gas units, the relief valve discharge and the pan and drain where one is needed. An inspector signs it off when the work meets code.

Homeowners sometimes see the permit as a cost to avoid. It is the opposite. It is the part of the job that gives you recourse if the install was wrong, and the part an insurer or a future buyer will ask about.

What the inspection looks at

  • Correct relief valve and a discharge line routed to a safe point.
  • A drain pan and drain line where the unit sits above finished space.
  • Venting sized and sloped correctly on gas units.
  • Seismic or strap requirements where they apply.
  • Correct electrical connection and bonding.

Most failed inspections we see come down to venting or the discharge line, both of which are safety items rather than paperwork.

Who pulls it

A licensed plumber pulls the permit and meets the inspector. That should be included in the quoted price rather than added afterwards, and it is a fair question to ask before you agree to the work.

Permitting rules vary by municipality across the county. Some cities run their own, others route through Palm Beach County, and that affects timing more than cost.

Why it matters later

Unpermitted work surfaces at the worst moment. It comes up during a sale, during an insurance claim after water damage, or when the next plumber opens a wall and finds the install was never signed off.

If your heater is being replaced because the tank failed, what a bottom leak means covers the decision you are making. If it is still sound, maintenance may buy you years.

Permits are part of a replacement, not an extra. Settle sizing and the tank or tankless question before the permit is pulled.

Frequently asked questions

Can I replace my own water heater?

A homeowner can pull a permit for their own residence in many Florida jurisdictions. The work still has to pass inspection, and gas and venting are where self installs usually fail.

How long does the permit add?

Usually not much. Most replacements are scheduled and inspected within a few days, and the unit is in service the same day it is fitted.

What if my heater was replaced without a permit?

It can often be resolved after the fact. Talk to a licensed plumber before a sale rather than during one.
