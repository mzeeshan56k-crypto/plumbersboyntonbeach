Before you call

Information That Makes Your Plumbing Call Faster

A two-minute call can produce an accurate price range if you have a few details ready. None of it is complicated.

For a drain or sewer problem

Tell us which fixtures are affected and whether more than one backs up at the same time. Multiple fixtures point to the main line rather than a single drain. Mention whether the line has been cleared before and roughly when, because repeat clearings change the recommendation from cleaning to inspection.

For a water heater

Read the data plate: gas or electric, tank capacity in gallons, and the serial number, which usually encodes the build year in the first four characters. That is enough to tell you whether repair or replacement is the better spend before anyone drives out.

For a leak

Note whether the water is visible or suspected, whether the meter moves with everything shut off, and whether any floor feels warm. Those three answers separate a fixture leak from a slab leak, and they determine which detection equipment we bring.

For a remodel or installation

Have the fixture model numbers and a rough layout, including whether anything is moving location. A toilet or shower that moves more than a short distance usually means cutting slab, and knowing that early affects the design.

If water is running right now

Do not wait to gather information. Call, and we will walk you through shutting off the main while a plumber is dispatched.

Boynton Beach is home base, and our trucks run daily through the surrounding Palm Beach County cities. Lantana, Hypoluxo and Ocean Ridge are minutes away. Travel is not billed separately. A job at a Delray Beach address is priced exactly the same as one on the next street over from our base.

For condominiums, HOAs and gated communities, tell us the building or community name when you call. We will arrange gate access and send licensing and insurance documentation to management in advance so the appointment is not turned away at the entrance.
