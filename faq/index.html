Plumbing FAQs for Boynton Beach Homeowners

Straight answers on pricing, response times, permits and the plumbing problems that come up most often in Palm Beach County homes. If your question is about a specific job, the detail usually sits on that service page: slab leak repair, drain cleaning and water heater repair each cover pricing, timing and what to expect.

01

Booking and response

How fast can a plumber get to my home?

Most Boynton Beach addresses are reached within 60 to 90 minutes. Neighboring towns like Lantana, Hypoluxo and Ocean Ridge are often faster. Wellington, being furthest west, may take a little longer unless you call early.

Do you answer the phone after hours?

Yes. A person answers (833) 567-2788 during business hours, and after-hours emergency calls are dispatched as they come in. You get an arrival window before you hang up.

Can I schedule for a specific day and time?

Yes. Tell us the window that works and we will book it. Morning slots fill first, so call ahead when the job is not urgent.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Clear access to the fixture or area, move anything valuable out of the way, and if water is running, shut off the main. We will walk you through the shutoff on the phone if you are not sure where it is.

02

Pricing and payment

Is your pricing really flat rate?

Yes. The plumber looks at the actual problem, writes a price, and you approve it before any work starts. There is no hourly meter and no line items added at the end.

Do you charge for estimates?

Estimates for scheduled installations and replacements are free. Diagnostic work, such as locating a hidden leak or finding out why a main line keeps backing up, carries a flat service fee that is credited toward the repair when you go ahead.

Why does emergency work cost more?

After-hours calls mean a plumber leaves whatever they were doing, at night or on a weekend. We say so plainly rather than hiding it in the invoice, and you still get a fixed price rather than an open-ended rate.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, all major credit cards, debit and digital wallets. A written invoice is emailed with parts, labor and warranty terms listed.

Do you offer any warranty?

Yes. Parts and labor coverage is printed on your invoice. Terms vary by service, and installed equipment also carries the manufacturer warranty.

03

Licensing and permits

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Yes. Every plumber dispatched holds a current Florida license and carries liability insurance. Ask for the license number at the door and verify it with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

Which jobs need a permit?

Water heater replacement, repiping, sewer line work, gas line installation and most remodel plumbing require permits in Boynton Beach and Palm Beach County. We pull them and meet the inspector.

What happens if work was done without a permit before?

It can create problems at resale or during an insurance claim. We can often bring older work up to code and permit it during a related job.

04

Common plumbing problems

My drain keeps clogging after being cleared. Why?

Because the clog was punched through rather than removed, or because the pipe itself is damaged. Old cast iron with heavy scale and roots at a joint both cause repeat backups. A camera inspection settles it.

What causes a slab leak?

Copper supply lines poured into concrete decades ago. Hard water attacks them from the inside, damp alkaline concrete from the outside, and thermal movement wears thin spots. Hot lines usually fail first.

Why is my water pressure dropping over time?

In older homes, galvanized pipe rusting closed from the inside. In newer homes, a failing pressure-reducing valve or scale in fixture cartridges and aerators.

Why does my water heater keep running out?

Sediment on the tank floor reduces usable capacity and insulates the burner. A flush often restores it. If the tank is over ten years old, replacement is usually the better spend.

Is a rotten egg smell dangerous?

If it is at a gas appliance, leave the house and call the gas utility from outside. If it is only in hot water, that is hydrogen sulfide, common on well water, and it is a treatment issue rather than a safety emergency.

05

Local conditions

Is Boynton Beach water hard?

Yes. Palm Beach County water commonly tests between 8 and 12 grains per gallon. Anything over 7 is considered hard, and it shortens water heater and fixture life noticeably.

Should I install a water softener?

If you are tired of scale on fixtures, replacing water heaters early and dealing with stiff laundry, yes. We test your water before recommending equipment.

Why do coastal homes have more fixture problems?

Salt air corrodes plated finishes and exposed brass. Solid brass bodies and stainless hardware last much longer east of Federal Highway.

Does the water table affect my plumbing?

It does. Boynton Beach sits low, so slab leaks spread further before they show, buried metal corrodes faster, and sewer laterals with cracked joints take on groundwater during the wet season.

Call and ask. We would rather talk you out of an unnecessary visit than sell you one.
